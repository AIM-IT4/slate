# Code of Conduct

Slate welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Give actionable feedback, credit other people's work, and respect privacy. Make room for newcomers and accessibility needs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private information without permission, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not spam issues or use the project to solicit stars through deception.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove or edit harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or limit participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and prioritize the people affected.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content. For a matter that needs a private project channel, request private contact from a maintainer without publishing personal details. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is unacceptable.
